# Deliberately minimal. This is a measurement instrument, not a deployment:
# every dependency is a potential source of nondeterminism in a study whose
# entire output is detection probability and false-alarm rate.
#

# adds a heavy dependency and actor-based scheduling for zero measurement
# benefit. FedAvg (McMahan et al., 2017) is implemented directly in
# fedassure/fedavg.py so that seeding, client selection, and aggregation are
# fully under our control and reproducible run-to-run.
